My Story

I've always been drawn to the way stories live — in words, images, communities, and shared moments, and especially through fashion. Every brand has a story to tell and requires a visual direction to communicate their mission.

I'm a digital marketer and creative producer with a passion for storytelling, community, and showcasing visual identity. I've worked across tech, fashion resale, and editorial spaces, where I've led social media strategy, brand campaigns, content creation and production, and brand communications.

Whether I'm shaping a content calendar, coordinating visual shoots, or helping a team roll out a campaign, I bring an organised, curious, and collaborative mindset to everything I do. I'm drawn to brands with a point of view — those that treat content as a craft, not just a channel.

My background in psychology informs how I think about audiences — understanding behaviour, building trust, and creating content that resonates on a human level. My MSc in Creative Industries grounds the strategic side: how brands create value, build communities, and sustain relevance.
