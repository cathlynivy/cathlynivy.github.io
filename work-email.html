Email

Crafting content layouts and communication strategies that guide audiences from discovery to conversion at key seasonal moments.

The Channel

Email marketing offers something social media can't — a direct, unmediated line to an already-engaged audience. Without algorithm interference, it becomes a space to tell a more layered brand story, build anticipation before key moments, and guide subscribers naturally toward purchase.

I believe email content should feel like a continuation of the brand world — not a separate, transactional channel. The best campaigns mirror the visual and editorial quality of the brand's other touchpoints, while being purpose-built to drive action.

Concept Project — Rohe

This concept was developed for Rohe, focusing on the Pre-Spring collection launch. The strategy covers two key moments: an anticipation email introducing the lookbook before it drops, and a conversion email communicating that the collection is now available to shop.

Communication Strategy

Lookbook introduction email — aspirational editorial imagery and considered copy to build desire before the drop date
Collection available email — clear CTA, product highlights, and natural urgency to drive conversion
Audience segmentation — tailoring tone and personalisation for new subscribers versus returning customers
Visual hierarchy designed to guide the eye from editorial storytelling through to product and purchase
Subject line strategy optimised for open rate — evocative, not transactional
