The Platform

Instagram is where people come to follow a brand's story, often the first place a curious new customer will visit to understand its identity, tone, and visual world. The profile grid, captions, and stories work together to communicate who a brand is, what it values, and why it matters.

At O My Bag Amsterdam, I led end-to-end social media management across Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest. My role was to translate the brand's mission, sustainable and handcrafted leather goods, into content that felt alive, resonant, and commercially effective.

I developed narrative arcs around product launches, seasonal moments, and cultural touchpoints. Stories, Reels, and grid posts were planned in concert, with each piece supporting the broader brand message while optimising for platform-specific behaviour.

I also launched and maintained the brand's Instagram Broadcast Channel, a direct community touchpoint that deepened connection with loyal followers and gave the brand a new layer of intimacy.

My Approach

I treat Instagram as both a creative medium and a strategic touchpoint. Content must stand on its own aesthetically, while also serving the commercial calendar. I use data from Sprout Social and native analytics to identify what resonates, then iterate, testing formats, captions, timing, and visual styles to continuously improve performance.
