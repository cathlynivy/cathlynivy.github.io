The Discipline

Balancing a content calendar takes meticulous planning and strategy, aligning campaigns across multiple platforms while considering timing, audience behaviour, and overall brand direction.

It's a process of weaving together seasonal moments, brand priorities, and creative narratives in a way that feels both timely and visually consistent. Each piece of content must not only stand on its own, but also support the broader story, whether it's a product launch, a cultural moment, or a long-term brand message.

I use Notion as my primary planning tool, building systems that give teams visibility across all platforms, track publishing status, and keep creative assets, copy, and scheduling information in one place. Across my roles at O My Bag and Defactor, I managed multi-platform calendars balancing social, email, and paid content simultaneously.

My Process

01
Brand & Campaign Mapping
Identify key dates, product launches, seasonal moments, cultural events and map campaign priorities across the quarter.
02
Platform Cadence Planning
Set publishing frequency and format mix per platform, accounting for algorithm behaviour and audience activity windows.
03
Creative Briefing
Brief content needs to design, photography, and copy teams with clear context, timeline, and platform specifications.
04
Review, Adapt & Report
Monitor performance weekly, adapt the calendar based on what's working, and report findings to inform the next planning cycle.
